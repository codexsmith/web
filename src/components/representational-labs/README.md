`RepresentationalLabShell` provides deliberately modest common infrastructure:

- experiment identity and question framing,

- reusable panel/status/trace primitives,

Shared infrastructure must be earned by cross-domain evidence. Do not promote one lab's local record shape or domain semantics into a universal suite contract until at least one additional domain demonstrates the same requirement.

## Cantor Closure & Defect

Route: `/sandbox/cantor`

The current v0.7 finite calibration fixture begins as a literal 4×4 representation. It distinguishes:

1. the full same-width family outside the represented list,
2. the subset certified by the current selector/exclusion rule,
3. selector obstruction from genuine finite-universe exhaustion,
4. row growth from coordinate-width growth,
5. the executable finite fixture from Cantor's established infinite theorem and BFL's experimental closure/defect interpretation.

The fixture is intentionally not presented as a proof of a novel transfinite theorem.

Current interaction capabilities include:

- binary and finite ternary grammars,
- editable represented rows,
- identity-diagonal and retargeted selectors,
- non-injective coordinate reuse once rows outnumber coordinates,
- visible forced / branching / obstructed selector regimes,
- explicit same-width outside-family counts,
- explicit width-growth breakpoint only when the finite same-width universe is exhausted,
- `STEP` for one internal `SCAN → ESCAPE → ADMIT` phase,
- `PLAY → NEXT CHANGE` for fast-forwarding to the next list-shape mutation,
- optional `BREAK ON ESCAPE` as an early debugger breakpoint, disabled by default,
- an always-manual `ADD COORDINATE` repair after `WIDTH GROWTH FORCED`,
- responsive and reduced-motion-aware presentation.

For the starting binary 4×4 fixture there are 16 possible four-bit rows, 4 are represented, and therefore 12 remain outside the list. Under the classical identity selector the residual family is rigid: one antidiagonal witness is certified. As members are admitted, the list may become 5×4, 6×4, and so on without widening. Only when all 16 four-bit rows are represented is same-width continuation impossible under that fixed alphabet/width contract.

The public interaction should feel like a small automaton or debugger rather than a research dashboard: STEP when inspecting mechanism; PLAY when advancing to the next consequential structural state.
