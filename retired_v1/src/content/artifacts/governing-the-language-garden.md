# Governing the Language Garden

**Document class:** Working public operations note  
**Version:** v0.1  
**Policy state:** Adopted for the local website implementation; founder approval remains open

## Purpose

The **world class / class of world / executable distinctions** language family is unusually generative. That is its value and its risk.

Without governance, a public line can become detached branding, a playful phrase can enter a claim box, a research hypothesis can sound like settled doctrine, or polished replacement language can erase its source.

The operating principle is therefore:

> **Every promoted phrase carries a declared meaning, intended context, source path, claim ceiling, known ambiguity, and replacement path.**

The garden remains alive because it can be pruned without losing its roots.

## Working canonical meaning

> **World class is not a status we claim. It is a capacity we give.**

The line directs attention away from prestige and toward public capacity, rigor, consequence, accessibility, repairability, answerability, and institutional stewardship.

It remains a recommended default pending founder approval. The registry does not silently convert that recommendation into a final decision.

## Registers

### Institutional

Mission, website, founder-letter, and institutional language may use the public-capacity meaning without technical explanation. Prestige-only or human-ranking readings remain prohibited.

### Software-native

Technical language may provide an on-ramp for software audiences. It must include enough structural explanation to show that the language concerns representation, state, interface, responsibility, and repair rather than a pun alone.

### Formal research

Terms such as **operational homology**, **shared grammar**, **projection**, and **class of possible worlds** require source definitions, an evidence level, preserved structure, breakpoints, claim status, and a next test.

### Public philosophy

Public formulations about words, categories, power, and governance require a reachable mechanism or explainer. The claim is never that words possess mystical force in isolation.

### Playful and campaign

Informal language may be used sparingly in social, event, merchandise, and workshop settings. It may not substitute for technical explanation and is prohibited in claim boxes, academic abstracts, and safety statements.

### Semantic firewall

Safeguards travel with the risks they constrain:

- **Class without caste. Distinction without domination.**
- **A person is not exhausted by any software or institutional representation.**
- **A useful analogy is not an established formal equivalence.**

## Editorial checklist

Before publication, record:

1. **Register and audience** — who will encounter the phrase and in which channel?
2. **Status and priority** — is it a candidate, recommended default, research hypothesis, restricted variant, safeguard, approved canonical line, or retired record?
3. **Meaning** — what institutional or operational meaning must remain attached?
4. **Provenance** — which source preserves the original contribution, and what role did founder language, editorial synthesis, research inference, or external evidence play?
5. **Claim ceiling** — what may the phrase communicate, and what does it not establish?
6. **Ambiguity and firewalls** — which foreseeable misreadings require a safeguard?
7. **Context rules** — which channels are permitted, which are prohibited, and which explainer must remain reachable?
8. **Replacement path** — what triggers review, and how will succession or retirement be recorded?

## Review gates

### Gate A — Copy review

Declare audience and register, retain the intended meaning, prevent hierarchy-of-worth readings, and expose the claim status and required explainer.

### Gate B — Research review

Provide source-domain definitions, mapping level, preserved structure, counterexamples, domain caveats, evidence, breakpoints, and next test.

### Gate C — Public promotion

Approve canonical wording or a channel-specific variant, provide a linked explainer, clear visual and source rights, and declare correction and replacement routes.

## Replacement without erasure

The register is append-only.

- Never silently rewrite a promoted phrase record.
- Give every successor a new identifier and a link to the record it replaces.
- Keep retired phrases discoverable with rationale, prior uses, and replacement route.
- Narrow channel permissions or meaning through a new governance decision, not retroactive source editing.

This is not bureaucratic ceremony. It is how the language system models the doctrine it expresses: boundaries remain declared, transitions are witnessed, consequence is traceable, and repair does not destroy provenance.

## Present claim ceiling

The operating policy governs the current local website and publication language. It tracks candidates, research hypotheses, campaign variants, safeguards, sources, review gates, and replacement paths.

It does not record final founder approval, prove the broader theories carried by the phrases, or authorize every phrase for every channel.
