## Audit result

The updated guide is paired to the current v0.3 webpage content graph rather than to the earlier institutional packet alone.

### Integrated recent revisions

- applied public-interest research institute identity;
- founder-led, AI-enabled micro-lab current stage;
- secured public moniker and formation-stage facts;
- staged mission, institutional aim, and bounded long horizon;
- preferred maxim: “One method. One living system. Many boundaries to repair.”;
- typed institutional covenant;
- institutional-closure-map projection;
- collaboration modes, role classes, lifecycle, public promise, evidence standard, rights, attribution, and closure;
- participation-to-collaboration routing;
- governance and collaboration filters in the Context Halo;
- product stewardship and closure gate;
- conventional and Boundary First projections from one source.

### Preserved earlier UX layers

- guided first passage;
- atlas;
- Context Halo;
- Historical Lineage lens;
- project/product type grammar;
- work and evidence projections;
- accessible conventional pages.

### Release boundary preserved

The guide does not substantively publish Self-Improving Representations or Agentic Scientific Method. It refers only to the bounded canonical long-horizon field and requires a separate promotion step before those programs appear in public content.

### Known remaining gaps

The canonical JSON does not yet provide complete structured records for:

- formal adoption of each institutional statement;
- named owners for every policy or commitment;
- evidence of operation;
- review cadence;
- known defects and remediation status;
- replacement history;
- live collaboration instances;
- complete first-class project and product objects in the canonical graph.

The UX must render those gaps honestly rather than infer completion.
