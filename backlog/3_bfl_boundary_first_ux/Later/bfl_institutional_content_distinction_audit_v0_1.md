## Governing rule

> A principle is not automatically a policy. A policy is not automatically adopted. Adoption is not implementation. Implementation is not evidence of successful operation.

The extended register preserves exact source wording. A smaller core view excludes general program context from the default UX dataset while preserving it in the full harvest and assigns conservative status assessments. Any status beyond what the source explicitly states remains unverified.

## Primary finding: the content is rich but normatively mixed

The present institutional material includes at least these different classes:

```text
identity and rationale
mission
vision
public principles and positions
governance doctrine and commitments
proposed institute policy
decision and review rules
publication and evidence gates
portfolio and stewardship requirements
participation and standing
criticism, correction, replacement, and withdrawal
```

These classes should not share one visual status. The future interface must distinguish what the lab **is**, **aims toward**, **declares**, **proposes**, **has adopted**, **has operationalized**, and **can demonstrate**.

These are not content defects to be silently filled. They are the next governance-design work.
