## Promotion blockers

A statement may not be publicly displayed as adopted or operating until the record includes evidence for:

1. formal approval or adoption;
2. scope and effective date;
3. named owner;
4. procedure or implementation mechanism;
5. review cadence;
6. correction and supersession path;
7. evidence that the mechanism is actually in use.
