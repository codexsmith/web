## Boundary First Labs Working Package v0.1

**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Status:** Internal doctrine, public-on-ramp, and research seed

## Purpose

This package preserves and operationalizes a conversation about institutional accounting, human value, delegated business agency, social distance, externality, and responsibility.

It separates:

- memorable public maxims;
- the Hostage and the Accountant parable;
- a bounded institutional doctrine;
- an analysis of B2B delegation and recursive value extraction;
- accounting and claim-boundary cautions;
- campaign, essay, visual, and teaching formats;
- a task and goal program for Boundary First Labs.

## Central working proposition

> **People make the value. People are not overhead.**

The fuller institutional hierarchy is:

> **People are the source. People are the destination. Institutions are the means.**

A business is a delegated agency structure built by people to coordinate capacities and produce value for people. Business-to-business activity is legitimate when one institution helps another fulfill that human-serving purpose. It becomes pathological when the delegation chain closes around institutional metrics, balance sheets, and extraction before value and consequence return to people.

## Claim ceiling

This package is a doctrine and research seed. It does not establish a complete economic theory, accounting standard, labor-law position, empirical claim about all firms, or universal moral theory of institutions. Technical accounting, economic, legal, and historical claims require targeted validation before publication as fact.
