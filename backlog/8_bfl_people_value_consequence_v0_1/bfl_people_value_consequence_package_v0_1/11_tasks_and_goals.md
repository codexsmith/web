## Program success criteria

The project succeeds when an ordinary person can use the framework to distinguish:

- a person from the category applied to a transaction;
- real cost reduction from burden relocation;
- productive B2B delegation from recursive institutional extraction;
- responsibility from symbolic ownership;
- empathy by proximity from durable representation;
- local efficiency from preserved human and systemic capacity;
- critique from an actionable repair route.
