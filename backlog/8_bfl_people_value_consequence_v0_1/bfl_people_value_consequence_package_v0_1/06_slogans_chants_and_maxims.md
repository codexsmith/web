# Slogans, Chants, and Maxims
## Public Language Inventory v0.1

## 1. Primary slogan

> **People make the value. People are not overhead.**

This is the strongest current public pair. It names both the source of value and the institutional category error.

## 2. Canonical hierarchy

> **People are the source. People are the destination. Institutions are the means.**

Best for doctrine cards, essays, talks, and institutional design.

## 3. Strong chant forms

### Three-line chant

```text
People make the value!
People bear the cost!
People are not overhead!
```

### Call and response A

```text
Who makes the value?
We make the value!

Who bears the cost?
We bear the cost!

What are we?
Not overhead!
```

### Call and response B

```text
People make it!
People need it!
People are the point!
```

### March cadence

```text
We make the value.
We carry the load.
We are the people.
We are not overhead.
```

### Institutional challenge

```text
No people, no value.
No care, no future.
No repair, no closure.
```

## 4. Compact maxims

- People are the point.
- People make the value.
- People are not overhead.
- Labor is part of the enterprise.
- Cost is not identity.
- A ledger is not a life.
- The books are not the world.
- Value must return to people.
- Institutions are means, not ends.
- No value without human capacity.
- No efficiency by exported harm.
- No savings by hidden depletion.
- No profit without repair.
- Responsibility must return.
- Distance does not erase consequence.
- The stranger is still inside the system.
- The rule must survive proximity.
- The agency chain must return to people.

## 5. Parable lines

- The numbers had not changed. Only the boundary of concern had moved.
- The rule was principled while it governed strangers.
- The accountant heard a familiar voice, and the books changed.
- What the ledger excludes, someone still lives.
- The hostage was never an externality.
- The accountant called the cost reasonable until the hostage became family.

## 7. Language recommendations

Use `people make the value` as the public lead.

Use `people are not overhead` as the category-error challenge.

Use `people are the source; people are the destination; institutions are the means` as the explanatory doctrine.

Use `the agency chain must return to people` for B2B and institutional design.

Use `the rule must survive proximity` for the social-distance and policy test.

Avoid using `labor is never a liability` as an unqualified technical accounting claim. Preserve it as a plain-language institutional statement only when followed by the distinction between a person, a cost, and an accrued obligation.
