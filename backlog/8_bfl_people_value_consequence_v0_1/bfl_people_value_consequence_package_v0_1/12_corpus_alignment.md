# Corpus Alignment
## Relationship to Existing Boundary First Labs Work

## 1. Institutional doctrine

This package extends the existing Boundary First Labs institutional principles rather than creating a separate moral system.

The closest existing principles are:

- **No consequence without representation.**
- **Responsibility must land.**
- **Repair is infrastructure.**
- **No hidden human shock absorbers.**
- **Our roots must balance our branches.**

The new cluster adds a specific labor, value, and business-agency interpretation:

> Affected people can be represented as cost categories while remaining absent as agents with standing, capacity, and repair claims.

## 2. Externality and accounting

The existing Civilizational Mechanics pathway states:

> An externality is not outside the system. It is outside the system's accounting.

The current package gives that proposition an embodied labor and institutional form. Wages, benefits, recovery, maintenance, care, injury, exception handling, and future repair can be represented narrowly or transferred into households, public systems, contractors, communities, and future workers.

## 3. Business as artificial agency

The existing corpus treats the corporation as a persistent constructed agent formed by contracts, accounts, policies, governance, employees, software, capital, and infrastructure.

The current package adds:

- business-to-business exchange as delegated second-order agency;
- agency depth as a consequence-risk variable;
- terminal human value and accountability;
- recursive value extraction when the chain closes around institutions.

## 4. Accounting meets software engineering

The existing pathway states that accounting declares what the system recognizes while software engineering integrates what operation cannot afford to forget.

The current package offers a concrete software-and-institutional test:

```text
Does the organization's operational reality require human judgment,
care, exception handling, recovery, maintenance, and repair that the
accounting or management representation treats as absent or disposable?
```

This makes `people are not overhead` an on-ramp to hidden edge cases and human shock absorbers.

## 5. Value and durable capacity

The Civilizational Mechanics reference identifies a severance between represented value and durable system capacity. It warns that benefits can be captured inside a valuation boundary while labor, infrastructure, ecology, trust, and future options are depleted outside it.

The current doctrine is a direct public compression of that mechanism:

> **People make the value. People are not overhead.**

## 6. Constructive Humanist Agentics

The package aligns with the principle that affected people must remain visible as agents rather than variables, users, costs, or targets.

The human terminus of a business chain should therefore include more than consumption. It should include:

- standing;
- capability;
- participation;
- contestability;
- consequence-bearing;
- repair;
- future possibility.

## 8. Non-duplication boundary

This package should not replace the broader Externality, Civilizational Mechanics, Institutional Principles, or Constructive Humanist Agentics documents.

Its unique contribution is the focused synthesis of:

```text
human value creation
    + accounting category error
    + hidden labor and capacity depletion
    + proximity-mediated concern
    + B2B delegated agency depth
    + terminal accountability
```

## 9. Promotion recommendation

Promote first as a **public doctrine and on-ramp cluster**, not as formal economic theory. Develop the audit instrument and case studies before claiming diagnostic superiority or broad empirical validity.
