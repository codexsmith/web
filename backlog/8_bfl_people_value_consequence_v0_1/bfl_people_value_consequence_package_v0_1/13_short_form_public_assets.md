# Short-Form Public Assets
## Ready-to-Refine Copy

## 1. Public position statement

People make the value. People are not overhead.

A wage can be an operating expense. An unpaid obligation can be recorded as a liability. But those categories describe a transaction from an institution's point of view. They do not define the human being.

The category error begins when a company claims the output of human capacity as its value while treating the conditions that sustain that capacity as alien burdens. Health, rest, care, safety, judgment, accommodation, maintenance, and repair become costs to eliminate. The resulting damage is moved into households, public systems, contractors, communities, and the future, then renamed efficiency.

People are the source. People are the destination. Institutions are the means.

## 2. Social post

A business can call wages an expense. It cannot honestly call people the problem.

People make the value. People carry the cost. People repair what the ledger forgets.

People are not overhead.

## 3. Hostage and Accountant micro-parable

The accountant approved the cost while the hostage remained a number.

Then the hostage spoke with the accountant's child's voice.

The rule changed.

The numbers had not changed. Only the boundary of concern had moved.

## 4. B2B explainer

B2B is not inherently detached from people. It is delegated service: one institution helps another institution serve people better.

The danger begins when the chain closes around businesses, metrics, and portfolios before value and responsibility return to people.

The agency chain must return to people.

## 5. Four-question card

Before calling something efficient, ask:

1. Who made the value?
2. Who received the value?
3. Who carried the cost?
4. Who can demand repair?

## 7. Public FAQ fragment

### Are you saying labor has no cost?

No. The claim is that a cost category is not a complete description of a person or of the role human capacity plays in an enterprise.

### Are you saying every B2B company is extractive?

No. B2B is often useful delegated service. It becomes structurally dangerous when the chain no longer returns to represented human benefit, standing, consequence, and repair.

### Are you against accounting?

No. Accounting is necessary. The problem is abstraction without return: a bounded representation becomes dangerous when its exclusions are forgotten while it continues to govern real lives.

### Why use a chant?

Because ordinary people need compact language that lets them recognize a complex category error. The chant opens the inquiry; it does not replace the analysis.
