## Outcome

This phase established a coherent local website system for expressing Boundary First Institute's language, research boundaries, evidence state, and visual reasoning. The G1-G4 sequence is implemented, original operational diagrams are available, and claim-bearing interfaces now expose reusable evidence context.

The remaining identity gap is explicit: the website has a capable full-screen entrance framework, but it does **not** yet implement the Cosmic Shore storyboard or visual mark described in `cosmic_shore.md` and the supplied four-frame concept image.

The local implementation is technically healthy. Editorial adoption, research review, Cosmic Shore production, hosting selection, and deployment remain separate decisions.

## Evidence-vitals design decision

The corpus and research figures are now expressed through a reusable component rather than isolated content bars. Its role is to keep claim context visible without turning every page into a dashboard.

Current behavior:

- full statistical context at wider viewports;
- a compact priority summary at smaller viewports;
- accessible expansion for the complete figures;
- consistent corpus and research definitions across claim-bearing pages; and
- an explicit reminder that figures provide context, not proof.

Current compact claim context:

- 630 source-stated records;
- 0 operationally verified records;
- 2 bounded cases; and
- 10 recorded breakpoints.

Placement should remain selective. The component belongs wherever the site discusses the strength, volume, provenance, or veracity of claims; it should not be repeated on purely navigational or low-claim pages.

## Cosmic Shore audit

### Finding

The source file exists at:

`Lab/organized_library_curated/02_Core_Identity/website_packet/cosmic_shore.md`

No source import, content reference, public asset, or implementation trace currently connects that file to the website. The present homepage entrance is therefore best described as **identity-splash infrastructure**, not as an implementation of Cosmic Shore.

### What the current entrance already provides

- a full-screen first-visit identity surface;
- staged entrance choices;
- a control that reveals all starting points immediately;
- session-aware return behavior; and
- reduced-motion handling.

### What remains absent

- the steward tending the earth;
- the Earth-like sphere reveal;
- furrows transforming into waves or contour lines;
- the Earth transforming into the Cosmic Shoreline emblem;
- the steward and field receding;
- the final `Boundary First Labs` lockup;
- the `Research · Repair · Stewardship` tagline;
- the approximately 2.5-second four-stage motion sequence shown in the supplied image; and
- any transition that carries the final mark into persistent site identity.

### Timing decision required

The supplied image specifies a concise approximately 2.5-second sequence:

1. **0.00-0.60 seconds:** the steward tends the earth.
2. **0.60-1.20 seconds:** an Earth-like sphere appears and rows become waves or contours.
3. **1.20-2.00 seconds:** Earth becomes the shoreline emblem while the steward and field recede.
4. **2.00-2.50 seconds:** the logo lockup and tagline hold.

The source document also contains a longer, more detailed storyboard of roughly 14-18 seconds. For a website entrance, the recommended v0.1 interpretation is the 2.5-second sequence. The longer treatment should remain an optional narrative or campaign asset rather than delay navigation.

## Cosmic Shore v0.1 acceptance checklist

- [ ] Four stages are visibly and semantically distinct.
- [ ] Default duration is approximately 2.5 seconds.
- [ ] Skip is available immediately and works with keyboard and pointer input.
- [ ] Returning visitors are not forced through the full sequence.
- [ ] Reduced-motion behavior avoids translation, morphing, and rapid reveal.
- [ ] The animation never blocks route access or essential content.
- [ ] The final lockup is readable at supported viewport sizes.
- [ ] The final identity name and tagline have recorded approval.
- [ ] New artwork has a documented rights and provenance record.
- [ ] Performance is checked on a representative low-power mobile profile.
- [ ] Static fallback remains intelligible if animation or scripting fails.
- [ ] Existing entrance, redirect, and route tests remain green.
