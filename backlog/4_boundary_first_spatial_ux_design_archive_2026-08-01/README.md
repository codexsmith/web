# Boundary First Labs Spatial UX and Corpus Forge Design Archive

**Archive date:** 2026-08-01  
**Status:** Operational design capture / pre-implementation package  
**Primary program:** Boundary First Labs public experience, Atlas, Corpus Forge, Binding Protocol Studio, Scientific Method Lens, spatial workbench, spatial pointer, and RTS-style research operations

## Purpose

This archive captures the complete substantive design thread in which the Boundary First Labs public website was reframed as an institutional entrance, Corpus Forge emerged as a governed research-production product, the Atlas became a guided knowledge environment, the binding-file protocol became a constructive UI system, and the entire experience expanded into an inhabitable spatial research environment with a boundary-aware pointer and RTS-like operational grammar.

The package is intended to support:

- product planning;
- feature decomposition;
- UI and interaction design;
- research-method specification;
- implementation sequencing;
- prototype construction;
- external communication;
- future Corpus Forge ingestion and promotion.

## Important scope note

`conversation/conversation_transcript.md` is a reconstructed, turn-by-turn **substantive transcript** from the visible conversation. It preserves every user design turn, the assistant's substantive proposals, and every generated concept artifact. It intentionally excludes hidden system messages, internal chain-of-thought, and low-level tool plumbing. It is therefore a complete design record rather than a raw platform export.

## Canonical decisions captured

- The public entrance is institutional and user-centered: **Who are you, why are you here, and what kind of place is Boundary First Labs for you?**
- Boundary First Labs is the institution; Boundary First is the method; Corpus Forge is the research-production and governance system; Boundary Theory is the theoretical foundation.
- Work in progress, proof attempts, conjectures, negative results, and failed approaches are publishable when claim status and claim ceiling remain explicit.
- The Atlas is an explorable knowledge environment with guided camera paths, not a static brochure or an edge-saturated graph.
- Corpus Forge communicates through durable artifacts, independent criticism, and human promotion gates.
- The binding-file protocol separates semantic research state from presentation and can generate multiple 2D and spatial interfaces from the same governed source.
- The familiar scientific method remains a recognizable inner cycle within a broader lawful inquiry cycle.
- The spatial environment must be functional, not decorative: rooms, objects, boundaries, and environmental states map to real artifacts, permissions, and workflows.
- The pointer is a boundary-aware spatial interaction field, not merely a flat screen coordinate.
- RTS interaction design is a useful operational grammar for scale, selection, queues, command, visibility, and ongoing research-state maintenance—but conquest incentives and fake urgency are explicitly rejected.

## Current maturity

This package is a **concept and requirements baseline**, not a validated product specification. The concept drawings establish intention, not final layouts. The spatial pointer and spatial epistemic environment are research hypotheses that require prototype testing.
