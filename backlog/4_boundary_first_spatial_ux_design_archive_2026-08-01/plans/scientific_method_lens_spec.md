# Scientific Method Lens Specification

## Product proposition

> Bring the scientific method you know. Corpus Forge binds it to sources, representations, alternatives, actions, traces, claims, failures, repair, and lawful reopening.

## Claim boundary

Corpus Forge does not replace proof, experiment, replication, peer review, domain expertise, or scientific institutions. It supersedes only thin linear workflow depictions by preserving the broader method-level machinery and persistent research state.

## Views

### Familiar View

```text
Observe → Ask → Research → Hypothesize → Test → Analyze → Conclude → Communicate
```

### Expanded Forge View

```text
Orient → Declare → Bind → Map → Constrain → Generate → Select
→ Act → Trace → Compare → Diagnose → Repair → Close → Preserve/Reopen
```

### Research-State View

Shows how the current cycle changes:

- source corpus;
- questions;
- candidate models;
- experiments/actions;
- raw traces;
- claims;
- contradictions;
- negative results;
- repairs;
- open obligations;
- promoted and retired artifacts.

## Required interaction

Selecting a familiar step highlights the corresponding expanded phases, artifacts, required declarations, gates, and incomplete obligations.

## Method profiles

- empirical hypothesis test;
- computational experiment;
- mathematical investigation;
- literature/evidence synthesis;
- engineering investigation;
- policy or institutional inquiry.

Each profile is a vocabulary and binding over one canonical lifecycle.

## Correspondence panel

```text
Represented                         Observed
Predicted state                    Registered state
Expected transition                Observed transition
Protected invariant                Measured signature
Declared tolerance                 Recorded uncertainty
Forbidden outcome                  Anomaly or absence
```

Mismatch produces a defect candidate, not an automatic model rejection. Defect locations include purpose, boundary, distinctions, model, action, instrument, trace, comparison rule, closure criterion, and authority.

## Lawful-cycle conditions

A cycle is lawful only when:

- purpose and boundary are declared;
- relevant states are represented;
- alternatives/null baseline exist where applicable;
- action selection has rationale;
- raw traces remain separate from interpretation;
- comparison criteria are versioned;
- mismatch is localized before repair;
- failed states remain preserved;
- claims remain within evidence and operating domain;
- promotion receives required criticism/authority;
- closure includes reopening conditions.
