## Purpose

This suite captures a connected line of development:

1. category and type errors as failures of representation;
2. social mediation displacing direct consequence as a dominant correction mechanism;
3. the resulting danger of representational reinforcement, including AI-mediated delusion;
4. the recovery of Lean, Agile, Scrum, and Kanban as bounded consequence-bearing repair circuits;
5. the conversion of those circuits into an AI-operable, instrumented repair loop;
6. a practical product and publication path for Boundary First Labs.

The governing proposition is:

> **Representation must not be allowed to declare itself correct. It must encounter an independent consequence channel, absorb discrepancy, repair the responsible structure, and earn closure.**

## Claim discipline

This package distinguishes among:

- **Conversation-grounded formulation:** a durable statement directly developed in the conversation;
- **Framework proposal:** an operational structure proposed for testing;
- **Interpretive hypothesis:** a broader social, cultural, or evolutionary interpretation requiring external research;
- **Product proposal:** a commercial packaging hypothesis requiring buyer validation;
- **Implementation specification:** a buildable design that still requires field testing.

No AI-generated artifact in this suite is self-validating. Promotion requires human review and, where relevant, external evidence, implementation, or market contact.
