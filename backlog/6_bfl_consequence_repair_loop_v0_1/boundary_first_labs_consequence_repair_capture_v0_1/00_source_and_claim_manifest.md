## Primary source

The primary source is the Boundary First Labs conversation in which the following development occurred:

- category-error detection was sharpened into the detection of illicit categorical transport;
- propaganda and manipulation were analyzed as truth or falsehood wearing an unearned category;
- a distinction was drawn between social evaluation and lawful consequence;
- social mediation was proposed as an intermediate selection environment that can delay, redistribute, obscure, or narratively repair consequence;
- AI-mediated delusion was diagnosed as representational reinforcement mistaken for consequential confirmation;
- Lean, Agile, Scrum, and Kanban were reconstructed as parts of a bounded consequence-bearing repair circuit;
- the circuit was specified such that AI can participate when consequence channels are explicit, independent, machine-readable, and auditable;
- a document, service, and software product path was identified.

## Claim classes and ceilings

### C1 — Structural framework claims

Examples:

- a claim, plan, model, or artifact is a representation rather than reality itself;
- completion is weaker than closure;
- an instrumented loop can compare expected and observed consequence;
- detection, diagnosis, repair, authorization, verification, and closure are distinct roles.

**Ceiling:** strong operational framework claim. These can be demonstrated in bounded software systems.

### C2 — AI engineering claims

Examples:

- AI can parse claims, generate tests, inspect telemetry, propose repairs, execute permitted changes, rerun verification, and produce audit records;
- AI can only participate lawfully to the extent that consequential feedback is available and authority is bounded.

**Ceiling:** implementation claim requiring system-specific validation. Do not state as universal autonomous capability.

### C3 — Social and institutional interpretation

Examples:

- many organizations reward social legibility over causal correctness;
- process rituals can become mediation layers that insulate teams from consequence;
- institutions may manage the representation of failure rather than repair its mechanism.

**Ceiling:** diagnostic hypothesis and case-study frame. Requires empirical examples and literature support for academic publication.

### C4 — Evolutionary interpretation

Example:

- human cultural evolution produced social mediation systems that can become partially autonomous selection environments.

**Ceiling:** conjectural interdisciplinary thesis. It must not be presented as an established biological-evolutionary result without research and qualification.

### C5 — Founder-history statements

Examples:

- early engagement with philosophy, physics, thought experiments, software, consulting, and iterative correction formed a lifelong constructive method;
- consulting formalized the who, what, why, when, where, and how of bounded problem search;
- software supplied rapid contact with execution and consequence.

**Ceiling:** autobiographical provenance. It explains origin, not validity.

### C6 — Product hypotheses

Examples:

- an AI Repair Loop Playbook can become a sellable document product;
- an AI Consequence-Loop Audit can become a consulting offer;
- the templates can become a software orchestration layer.

**Ceiling:** market hypothesis. Requires customer interviews, paid pilots, and conversion evidence.

## Protected distinctions

The following distinctions must survive reuse:

- representation / represented reality;
- coherence / evidence;
- review / consequence;
- completion / closure;
- feedback / repair;
- repair / authorization;
- agent capability / legitimate authority;
- social validation / causal validation;
- founder provenance / proof;
- analogy / derivation;
- framework / empirically validated method.

## Promotion requirements

A claim may move toward public or canonical status only when:

1. its source and class are explicit;
2. the operating domain is declared;
3. the claim is no stronger than its evidence;
4. failure conditions are stated;
5. contradictory cases remain visible;
6. any AI-generated support is distinguished from independent support;
7. the next test or consequence channel is specified.
