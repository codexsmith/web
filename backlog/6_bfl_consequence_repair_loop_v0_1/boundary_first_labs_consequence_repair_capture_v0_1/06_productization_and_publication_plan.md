# Productization and Publication Plan

## Productization rule

> **One buyer. One expensive failure. One bounded intervention. One visible consequence.**

The underlying theory is general. The first product must be narrow enough to purchase and verify.

## First document product

# The AI Repair Loop Playbook

**Subtitle:** How to keep AI-generated work open until reality verifies it

### Buyer

- CTOs;
- engineering leaders;
- AI platform teams;
- software consultancies;
- technical founders;
- regulated or high-consequence product teams.

### Pain

Teams are automating generation faster than they are automating correction. AI output can pass through model critique and human review without encountering an independent consequence channel.

### Promise

Provide a practical method and templates for turning AI-assisted development into an auditable repair loop in which:

- claims are typed;
- assumptions are visible;
- output encounters tests and telemetry;
- discrepancies become owned repair work;
- authority is bounded;
- closure is independently earned.

### Bundle

1. 25–40 page field guide;
2. Consequence-Loop Audit worksheet;
3. Claim Contract template;
4. Repair Job template;
5. AI Repair Authority matrix;
6. Closure Record template;
7. consequence-flow board states;
8. one complete software case study;
9. executive scorecard;
10. implementation checklist.

## Second document product

# Consequence-Bearing Development

**Subtitle:** Recovering Lean, Agile, Scrum, and Kanban as systems for matching representation to reality

This is a broader practitioner essay or short book. It can be positioned as the next development of *Closure-Driven Software Development* rather than a competing framework.

## Conceptual or academic paper

# From Social Mediation to Consequence Closure

**Subtitle:** Representation, AI reinforcement, and instrumented repair

### Central problem

Social and computational mediation can increase representational coherence while weakening or delaying contact with consequence.

### Proposed contribution

A typed framework for distinguishing:

- social validation from causal validation;
- representational reinforcement from independent confirmation;
- review from consequence;
- iteration from repair;
- completion from closure;
- capability from authority.

### Claim ceiling

This should begin as a conceptual synthesis and research agenda. Evolutionary and social claims require literature review and case evidence before strong academic presentation.

## First service product

# AI Consequence-Loop Audit

### Scope

Audit one AI-assisted software workflow, product slice, agent process, or repository.

### Questions

- Where can AI output be accepted without independent evidence?
- Where is `done` weaker than closure?
- Which consequence channels exist, and which are missing?
- Who owns discrepancy and repair?
- What may the AI detect, propose, execute, verify, and close?
- Which costs are displaced to users, operations, security, or future maintainers?

### Deliverables

- current-state consequence map;
- false-closure and recursive-confirmation findings;
- authority matrix;
- target repair-loop architecture;
- prioritized implementation backlog;
- one instrumented vertical-slice specification;
- executive readout.

### Pilot pricing hypothesis

Do not publish a permanent rate until buyer discovery is performed. Test a fixed-fee pilot with a sharply bounded scope and a paid implementation option.

## Market validation sequence

1. Interview five engineering or AI leaders about false completion and verification gaps.
2. Show the one-page audit offer.
3. Ask for one real workflow, not general feedback.
4. Run one paid or strongly committed pilot.
5. Record before/after circuit states and defects found.
6. Convert the pilot into a case study.
7. Revise the playbook from observed friction.
8. Build software only around repeated, demonstrated needs.

## Commercial language

Preferred:

> **Your AI can generate. We make sure it can detect failure, repair it, and prove closure.**

> **We help software teams turn AI generation into auditable repair loops.**

Avoid leading with the full theory. The theory should explain and differentiate the offer after the buyer recognizes the failure.
