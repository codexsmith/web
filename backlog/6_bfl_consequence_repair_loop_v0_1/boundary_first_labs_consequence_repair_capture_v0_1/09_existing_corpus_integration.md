# Existing Corpus Integration

## Placement in the Boundary First Labs stack

This capture should not create a parallel doctrine. It sharpens and connects existing work.

## Relationship to Closure-Driven Software Development

*Closure-Driven Software Development* already establishes:

- software projects begin before implementation;
- Boundary-First Engineering extracts domain boundaries and invariants;
- a delivery skeleton tests whether a real path can close;
- Agile is an observational system rather than a productivity ritual;
- interfaces protect invariants;
- leadership protects boundaries and truthful negotiation.

The present work adds:

- a more explicit consequence circuit;
- a typed distinction between social mediation and consequence mediation;
- independent consequence channels;
- discrepancy and repair as first-class workflow states;
- AI participation and authority boundaries;
- false-closure and recursive-confirmation diagnostics.

Recommended hierarchy:

```text
Closure-Driven Software Development
  └─ Consequence-Bearing Development doctrine
       └─ Bounded Consequence Circuit protocol
            └─ AI Repair Loop implementation
```

This preserves the existing publication while making the new work an operational deepening.

## Relationship to AI as Forge

The forge work emphasizes:

- synthesis without source replacement;
- folding, tempering, provenance, grain, and claim discipline;
- preserving what survives across repeated transformations;
- human judgment and closure gates.

The AI Repair Loop gives the forge an execution-facing consequence circuit. It can become the software and operations complement to the publication metaphor.

Potential integration:

- provenance is the tang;
- claim discipline is the temper;
- consequence channels are the testing surface;
- repair cycles are repeated forging passes;
- closure is release after demonstrated fitness, not aesthetic completion.
