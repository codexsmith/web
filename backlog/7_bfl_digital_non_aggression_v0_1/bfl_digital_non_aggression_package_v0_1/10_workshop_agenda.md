# Expert Workshop Agenda
## Digital Non-Aggression / Non-Destruction / Non-Interference

**Duration:** 2 hours  
**Mode:** Adversarial review and architecture workshop  
**Primary output:** Critique ledger and v0.2 revision requirements

## Pre-read

- Concept Brief
- Boundary-First Analysis
- Claim and Evidence Ledger
- Morris-II paper abstract and methods summary
- Adaptive AI-agent worm paper abstract and methods summary

## Participants sought

- cybersecurity researcher;
- incident responder;
- AI-agent engineer;
- international-law or cyber-law scholar;
- technology-policy researcher;
- civil-liberties or digital-rights advocate;
- infrastructure or platform operator;
- standards or certification practitioner.

## Agenda

### 0:00–0:10 — Frame and claim boundary

- What has been technically demonstrated?
- What is interpretation?
- What is proposed doctrine?
- What is explicitly not being claimed?

### 0:10–0:30 — Threat mechanics

- Morris-II propagation chain;
- adaptive-worm propagation chain;
- data/control collapse;
- victim-resource capture;
- limits of centralized controls.

**Challenge question:** Which claimed novelty is real, and which is old security language in a new wrapper?

### 0:30–0:50 — Three-part prohibition

- non-aggression;
- non-destruction;
- non-interference.

**Challenge question:** Are these categories mutually useful, legally dangerous, redundant, or incomplete?

### 0:50–1:10 — Responsibility and delegation

- principal;
- developer;
- provider;
- deployer;
- operator;
- platform;
- agent;
- intermediary;
- affected party.

**Challenge question:** Where should responsibility land when the behavior is adaptive, emergent, accidental, or difficult to attribute?

### 1:10–1:30 — Exceptions and abuse risks

- research;
- defense;
- emergency containment;
- law enforcement;
- open source;
- privacy;
- surveillance;
- censorship;
- monopoly enforcement.

**Challenge question:** How could this framework be weaponized by states or dominant platforms?

### 1:30–1:45 — Technical controls and verification

- provenance;
- instruction/data separation;
- least privilege;
- propagation controls;
- stop and revocation;
- audit and repair;
- recertification.

**Challenge question:** Which controls are realistically testable, and which are aspirational?

### 1:45–1:55 — Institutional form

Rank:

- protocol;
- standard;
- certification;
- compact;
- model law;
- convention;
- treaty.

### 1:55–2:00 — Closure

Record:

- strongest objection;
- strongest surviving contribution;
- claims to remove;
- definitions to repair;
- missing stakeholder;
- next test;
- go / revise / stop recommendation.

## Required workshop outputs

- issue ID;
- severity;
- affected concept or document;
- critic’s reasoning;
- supporting source or example;
- required repair;
- unresolved disagreement;
- owner;
- due stage;
- promotion impact.
