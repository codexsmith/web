# Civilizational Mechanics Doctrine Brief v0.1

> Civilization is an executable representation that must learn to revise itself before it fails beneath its own model.

## Public mission

Boundary First Labs develops the representational mechanics civilization needs to recognize its consequences, repair its institutions, and evolve before it fails beneath its own models.

## Root pattern

> capacity to act - required return path = exported consequence

## Agency and Consequence

> Power becomes dangerous when it can act upon bodies and places without remaining attached to either.

## Representation and Reality

> A representation becomes a defect when it forgets what reality still requires.

## Authority and Standing

> No one should be bound by a decision they have no meaningful way to contest.

## Responsibility and Capacity

> Responsibility becomes cruelty when it lands without authority, knowledge, resources, or repair capacity.

## Construction and Stewardship

> Nothing is truly built until maintenance, repair, replacement, and retirement are accounted for.

## Value and Durable Capacity

> Value is false when gain is counted here and damage is carried elsewhere.

## Local Success and Systemic Closure

> A system has not succeeded when its parts win by making the whole less viable.

## Legibility and Reciprocity

> Power should not see downward more clearly than it can be seen upward.

## Language and Operational Meaning

> A word becomes an instrument of capture when its public meaning and operative meaning diverge.

## Knowledge and Provenance

> Knowledge without a return path to witness becomes rhetoric with inherited authority.
