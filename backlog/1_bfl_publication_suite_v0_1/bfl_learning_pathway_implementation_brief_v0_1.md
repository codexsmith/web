# From Externality to Civilizational Repair - Learning Pathway v0.1

Civilization acts through executable representations. Boundary First Labs develops the mechanics needed to keep those representations answerable to embodied consequence and capable of lawful revision.

## Canonical spine

- **There Is No More Outside** - Every externality is someone else’s internality.
- **Externality Is a Boundary Choice** - An externality is not outside the system. It is outside the system’s accounting.
- **Business Is Already Artificial Agency** - The corporation is the paper-speed ancestor of the artificial agent.
- **AI Accelerates Institutional Agency** - A business is already an artificial agent. AI gives it a faster nervous system.
- **Abstraction Without Return** - The defining failure of the modern age is not abstraction itself. It is abstraction without return.
- **Accounting Meets Software Engineering** - Accounting declares what the system recognizes. Software engineering integrates what operation cannot afford to forget.
- **Boundary First** - Begin with the consequential boundary, what must remain true, where closure can fail, and who can repair it.
- **Every Baseline Opens a Field of Variation** - Variation must cohere into a new baseline, restore the old one, or destroy the conditions that made it possible.
- **Representational Evolution** - A system survives by evolving its representations before reality breaks through them.
- **Civilizational Mechanics** - Civilization is an executable representation that must learn to revise itself before it fails beneath its own model.
- **Route the Defect to Consequence Closure** - Critique becomes useful when the severed return path is made explicit and repairable.
- **Keep the Future Open** - Boundary First Labs develops the representational mechanics civilization needs to recognize its consequences, repair its institutions, and evolve before it fails beneath its own models.

## D3 visualization registry

- **There Is No More Outside**: `d3-hierarchy` / `partition` - Nested rectangles make the no-floor claim literal: each apparent outside is visibly inside a larger enclosure. A transfer path shows that consequence crosses rather than vanishes.
- **Externality Is a Boundary Choice**: `d3-sankey` / `sankey` - A Sankey diagram shows that reducing a represented cost does not erase flow; it redirects burden into another node.
- **Business Is Already Artificial Agency**: `d3-force` / `constrained force simulation` - A constrained network reveals that the business is neither one person nor an inert container; it is a persistent loop composed of human, legal, financial, and computational parts.
- **AI Accelerates Institutional Agency**: `d3-force` / `reuse business-agent coordinates` - Keeping the same graph while changing rate makes the thesis legible: the category of agent remains, but the cadence and bypass risk change.
- **Abstraction Without Return**: `d3-hierarchy` / `radial cluster` - A radial root structure makes the shared origin visible while preserving distinct branches. It supports card expansion without pretending the roots are identical.
- **Accounting Meets Software Engineering**: `d3-dag` / `sugiyama` - A layered DAG shows the exact professional move: a thin normative path becomes a richer executable state space with explicit failure and repair.
- **Boundary First**: `d3-shape` / `radial state cycle` - The radial cycle communicates recurrence and repair while direct stage selection supports operational learning.
- **Every Baseline Opens a Field of Variation**: `d3-hierarchy` / `icicle/partition` - The combined hierarchy and ordered progression shows that a new level becomes a substrate without ceasing to depend on the levels beneath it.
- **Representational Evolution**: `d3-shape` / `radial line / spiral` - A spiral preserves recurrence while showing that successful repair changes the baseline rather than returning to the identical starting point.
- **Civilizational Mechanics**: `d3-hierarchy` / `partition` - Nested layers show substrate dependence; cross-layer edges show that laws, accounts, software, and organizations execute across boundaries rather than staying inside one level.
- **Route the Defect to Consequence Closure**: `d3-sankey` / `sankey` - The Sankey view makes many-to-many routing visible: one root can produce several consequences, and one repair route can address several roots without collapsing their distinctions.
- **Keep the Future Open**: `d3-force` / `curated semantic coordinates with collision simulation` - The same nodes remain in place while context expands, preserving spatial memory and proving that the sequence and atlas are projections of one graph.
