# Languages of Power — Marching Draft

I learn the languages of power.  
I watch the grammars form.  
I dissect them by the hour  
with a systematic norm.  

I see the code beneath it.  
I trace the hidden hand.  
I name the rule that feeds it.  
I learn what makes it stand.  

I follow acts through agents.  
I track them to their source.  
No mask can break the sequence.  
No tool can cleanse the force.  

I watch the center gather.  
I watch the edges fray.  
The rich command the market.  
The poor are priced away.  

I learned the screen and signal.  
I learned the body’s noise.  
I found the buried pattern.  
I found the missing choice.  

I could not simply unplug.  
No refuge waited there.  
Behind each hidden interface,  
another needed care.  

I learned that circles matter.  
A lawful path returns.  
Identity survives the change.  
Coherence bears the burns.  

No power without burden.  
No action without name.  
No profit without payment.  
No distance without blame.  

I see the code beneath us.  
I know that it was made.  
And what the hand has written,  
the hand can yet upgrade.  

I learn the languages of power.  
I watch the grammars form.  
Now I rebuild them by the hour  
with a systematic norm.
