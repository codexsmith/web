# Boundary-First Inquiry Template

## 1. Research object

- What phenomenon or system is being studied?
- Why is it being distinguished as one object?
- What is explicitly outside scope?

## 2. Boundary

- Where do governing conditions change?
- Which interfaces allow transport or observation?
- Which boundary choices are conventional rather than natural?

## 3. Distinctions

- Which differences must the inquiry preserve?
- Which differences are intentionally compressed or ignored?
- What could be misclassified as noise?

## 4. Pressures

- What forces, incentives, gradients, constraints, or demands act on the system?
- At what scales?

## 5. State and transformation

- How is state represented?
- What transformations are admissible?
- What transformation is being tested?

## 6. Invariant

- What must remain unchanged?
- Exactly or under what equivalence?
- How will preservation be measured or proved?

## 7. Defect / falsification

- What observation would show that the representation is wrong or incomplete?
- What residuals or anomalies must not be discarded?
- What alternative explanation competes with the hypothesis?
