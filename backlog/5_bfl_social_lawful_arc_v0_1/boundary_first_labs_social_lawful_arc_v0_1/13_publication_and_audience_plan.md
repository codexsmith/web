# Publication and Audience Plan

## Public sequence

The material should not be released as one total theory. It should move from immediate lived recognition toward formal architecture.

### Essay 1 — Lawful Reconstruction

**Working title:** *The Alternative to Oppressive Order Is Better Order*  
**Audience:** general public, civic readers, funders.  
**Core:** lawful structure versus coercion; maintenance; social too-big-to-fail.

### Essay 2 — The Ransom Called Stability

**Audience:** public economics, antitrust, institutional readers.  
**Core:** “too big to allow to fail” as monopoly and dependency concentration.

### Essay 3 — Wealth Purchases the Shape of the Market

**Audience:** economics, housing, policy, general public.  
**Core:** minimum operating floor, weighted demand, second-order inequality.

### Essay 4 — Stop Calling Them Externalities

**Audience:** accounting, business ethics, policy, public-interest technology.  
**Core:** consequence accounting and closed profit.

### Essay 5 — Products That Fail Before Their Debts Do

**Audience:** consumers, engineering, finance, regulation.  
**Core:** shareholder maximization, financing, durability, transferred risk.

### Essay 6 — The Internet Laundered Agency

**Audience:** AI governance, platform policy, civil society.  
**Core:** communication as social substrate; compositional responsibility.

### Essay 7 — Speech May Deserve Privacy; Power Requires Provenance

**Audience:** privacy, civil liberties, platform governance.  
**Core:** anonymity dilemma, pseudonymity, counterfeit plurality, due process.

### Essay 8 — The First Lawful Move

**Audience:** educators, students, interdisciplinary researchers.  
**Core:** common primitive grammar as on-ramp to formalism.

### Essay 9 — Before the Hypothesis

**Audience:** philosophy of science, research methods, systems disciplines.  
**Core:** Boundary-First as a representational control layer for scientific inquiry.

### Essay 10 — Seeing the Code

**Audience:** founder narrative, public philosophy, Boundary First Labs identity.  
**Core:** digital denizen, double interface, provenance, artificer rather than hacker.

## Audience modes

### General public

Lead with concrete consequence and plain language. Introduce no more than four terms at a time.

### Practitioner

Lead with instruments: ledger, audit, worksheet, case study, repair path.

### Academic

Lead with definitions, existing literature, claim boundaries, mappings, and falsifiability.

### Policy/legal

Translate slogans into procedural entitlements, duties, limiting principles, and jurisdictional questions.

### Community/civic

Show what the framework lets affected people document, contest, preserve, or repair now.

### Founder/funder

Present a coherent institution with separable products and evidence surfaces rather than a requirement to accept the entire theory.

## Content derivatives

Each essay should generate:

- an 800–1,500 word public version;
- a 1-page brief;
- a diagram;
- a glossary card;
- a worked example;
- a claim-status box;
- a discussion or classroom prompt;
- a longer sourced research note.

## Public naming stack

- **Boundary First Labs** — institution and public vehicle.
- **Distinction Theory** — broad theoretical program.
- **Boundary Theory** — boundary as unifying object and structure of distinction spaces.
- **Representational Mechanics** — discipline for formalization, testing, and application.
- **Boundary First** — operational method.

## Publication rule

The public doorway should lead with clarity, concrete failures, and usable tools. The full cathedral remains available behind it.
