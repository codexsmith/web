# Boundary First Labs Visual Identity Catalog — Refined Working Edition

## 0. Core brand principle

Boundary First Labs needs a visual identity that can survive across three registers:

1. **The Digital Feral** — raw, nocturnal, dangerous, alive, glitch-intelligent.
2. **The Statesman** — civic, institutional, composed, accountable, public-facing.
3. **The Systems Architect** — rigorous, technical, clean, professional, engineering-grade.

They should not look identical. They should look like siblings produced by the same grammar.

The shared grammar:

> boundary + capsule + cut + invariant point + controlled defect

The Capsule Corp inspiration is useful because it is simple, iconic, and immediately readable at small sizes: a contained mark, a bold monogram, a playful technical seal. The move is not to copy it. The move is to learn from it: compact geometry, high recognizability, and a mark that can live equally well on a lab coat, website, interface, product label, or machine.

## 1. Master identity system

### Master mark: The Boundary Capsule

A rounded capsule or circle containing a minimal glyph:

* outer capsule = contexture / container / domain
* inner cut = boundary / distinction
* small dot or node = invariant / witness
* slight offset, notch, or fracture = defect / residue
* optional diagonal line = transport / crossing / emergence

The mark should work as:

* favicon
* avatar
* social icon
* document seal
* watermark
* slide corner mark
* sticker
* repo/org logo
* app icon
* Substack/publication avatar

### Possible master glyph directions

#### A. The Capsule-Cut Mark

A rounded capsule with a clean diagonal cut and a single invariant dot.

Feel: iconic, DBZ-adjacent without imitation, tech-company clean.

Best for: umbrella Boundary First Labs identity.

#### B. The Boundary Eye

A circular boundary with an inner slit/cut and a witness point.

Feel: observer, interface, perception, “lawful mirror.”

Best for: public essays, pedagogy, AI-human interaction.

#### C. The Contexture Node

A circle/capsule containing three connected nodes, one crossing a boundary line.

Feel: systems, graph, computation, research institute.

Best for: technical docs, software, AI alignment, research maps.

#### D. The Defect Seal

A clean circle with a deliberate notch, missing wedge, or offset line.

Feel: rigorous, minimal, powerful.

Best for: academic/formal artifacts.

#### E. The Forge Capsule

A capsule containing a spark, anvil line, or heated fold.

Feel: AI as Forge, craft, corpus forging, provenance, temper.

Best for: AI/craft/public writing lane.

## 2. The three faces

### Role

This is the mythic, nocturnal, high-voltage face.

It says:

> This work was not born in committee. It was hunted, forged, and survived.

### Audience

* online readers
* Substack hooks
* personal author avatar
* high-energy essays
* AI/craft lineage
* “Codex Smith” persona
* digital-native/transhuman/dev-art contexts

### Visual language

* dark background
* electric accent
* glitch edges
* sharp cuts
* feral but disciplined
* asymmetry allowed
* motion trails
* scanline / terminal / CRT / cyber-forge texture

### Logo variations

1. **Feral Capsule**

   * black capsule
   * electric cyan or violet cut
   * one red/orange invariant dot
   * slight glitch offset

2. **Codex Fang**

   * angular C/S monogram
   * one edge resembles a fang, claw, or broken bracket
   * not horror; more “wild intelligence under constraint”

3. **The Witness Mark**

   * one bright dot inside a dark boundary
   * boundary line cracked but not broken
   * conveys “I saw the defect”

4. **Forge Glitch Seal**

   * circular/capsule seal
   * inner fold line like heated metal
   * pixel distortion at one edge

* headings: sharp grotesk, technical sans, or condensed cyber sans
* body: readable sans
* accent: monospace for labels, equations, fragments

### Copy tone

* short
* sharp
* aphoristic
* survival-tested
* “blade doing the cutting” energy, but not incoherent

## 3. Shared iconography library

### Core icons

1. **Boundary**

   * line, ring, capsule wall, interface seam

2. **Defect**

   * notch, crack, offset, missing wedge, unresolved node

3. **Invariant**

   * fixed dot, protected center, stable node

4. **Closure**

   * completed loop, sealed capsule, checked boundary

5. **Transport**

   * arrow crossing a boundary, diagonal path, bridge line

6. **Projection**

   * shadow, map-fold, compressed outline

7. **Gauge**

   * ruler tick, dial, measurement frame

8. **Contexture**

   * nested container, graph environment, field of nodes

9. **Forge**

   * fold, spark, heat line, billet, hammer-strike abstracted

10. **Witness**

* dot/eye/node at a boundary

11. **Claim ceiling**

* horizontal limit line, capped column, bounded arrow

12. **Admissibility**

* gate, check seal, allowed crossing

### Icon style rules

* Same stroke width across all icons.
* Same rounded geometry as master capsule.
* Defects should be intentional, not messy.
* Every icon should work at 24px, 64px, and 256px.
* Avoid complex symbolic overload.
* Prefer “one concept per icon.”

## 9. Design constraints

* Do not copy Capsule Corp directly.
* Avoid dragon/anime literalism unless used privately as inspiration.
* Avoid occult geometry vibes for the public face.
* Avoid too many names on first contact.
* Avoid marks that only work large.
* Avoid unreadable mathematical detail in logos.
* Avoid making the statesman face boring.
* Avoid making the feral face unserious.
* Avoid making the technical face sterile.
