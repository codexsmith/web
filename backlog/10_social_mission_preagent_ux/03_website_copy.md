## Collaboration page

### Collaboration through complementarity

Boundary First Labs collaborates where there is a genuine division of comparative advantage. We do not seek collaborators merely for association, visibility, or borrowed legitimacy. We seek people and institutions that possess something essential we cannot reasonably obtain, should not attempt to reproduce, or deliberately do not want to build ourselves: domain authority, practical experience, implementation capacity, distribution, infrastructure, institutional access, capital, or stewardship. In return, BFL brings developed bodies of work, original representational systems, research methods, prototypes, and product directions that collaborators do not already possess and would face substantial uncertainty or expense in recreating independently.

A good collaboration begins with two explicit questions:

- What does Boundary First Labs bring that the collaborator does not already have?
- What does the collaborator bring that Boundary First Labs cannot or should not build alone?

Our objective is the smallest lawful relationship through which the work can be evaluated, improved, applied, distributed, funded, or placed with the party best equipped to carry it forward.

> We collaborate not because we cannot proceed alone, but because proceeding alone would require us to become something the work does not need us to be.

## Method page

### Boundary-First Systems

Boundary-First Systems is BFL's stewarded house method. It can be studied, practiced, imitated, trained, and eventually certified. Its canonical development, deepest corpus, critic protocols, and frontier practice remain with the originating lab.

The moat is not secrecy. It is accumulated method depth.

The method asks:

- What is being distinguished?
- Where is the boundary?
- What may cross it, and under whose authority?
- What must remain invariant through transformation?
- Where do consequences land?
- How is failure detected, contested, reversed, or repaired?

## Mathematics and physics notice

### Scope requires discipline

BFL maintains active mathematical and physical research programs, but these domains carry a heightened claim threshold. Structural resemblance is not proof. Interpretation is not prediction. A conjecture is not a result. Work in these areas is released through translation, comparison with known literature, counterexample search, replication, known-limit checks, and qualified external review.

## Founder page

### Built in the margins. Ready for the center.

I built Boundary First Labs while working other jobs and carrying the ordinary demands of family, health, household maintenance, and financial survival. The work advanced through nights, weekends, interrupted stretches, and whatever attention remained after immediate responsibilities were met. Funding would not create the underlying capacity or begin the research from zero. That capacity is already demonstrated in the work itself. Funding would convert fragmented, precarious effort into sustained execution: completing and publishing the strongest artifacts, running external validations, building the software infrastructure, recruiting the right collaborators, and turning a decade-spanning body of work into a durable institution. The question is not whether I can produce under ideal conditions. I have already produced under conditions far from ideal. The question is what becomes possible when the person who built all of this in the margins is finally given the means to work at the center.

> Boundary First Labs is not a promise of what I might do with funding. It is evidence of what I have already done without it.
