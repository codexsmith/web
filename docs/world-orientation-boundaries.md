# World orientation boundary vocabulary

These statements define the concise traversal-facing purpose of the primary public regions and their immediate contained regions. They are not replacements for the richer object summaries.

## Primary regions

- Products — What the Lab builds, ships, pilots, or deliberately keeps at concept status.
- Public Interest — Where technical capacity is directed toward public legibility, accountability, and repair.
- Publications — Where research and doctrine become versioned public artifacts with explicit maturity.
- About — Where institutional identity, method, provenance, and contact remain inspectable.
- Research — Where claims, methods, mechanisms, and formal structure are developed and tested.

## Products

- Current Work — Active development and bounded pilot surfaces.
- Shipped Work — Delivered systems retained as historical evidence, not current affiliation.
- Product Pipeline — Developed or planned product concepts without a shipped claim.
- Tools & Experiments — Small probes used to test representations before promotion.

## Public Interest

- Mission — What public purpose the Lab is organized to serve.
- Principles — Commitments that constrain how public-purpose work is carried out.
- Augusta Civic Infrastructure — A bounded civic-infrastructure direction grounded in prior delivery and current evidence.
- Goals & Aspirations — Desired future capacity separated from present capability claims.

## Research

- Software — Executable engineering doctrine: representation, architecture, interaction, verification, and governance.
- Applied Testbeds — Bounded domains used to test whether the method survives contact with specific reality.
- Foundations — Primitive objects and distinctions used to reconstruct the deeper formal program.
- Formal Theory — Generalization layer where the strongest abstractions carry the highest proof burden.

## Publications

- Essays & Arguments — Public-facing arguments and doctrine, with claim ceilings kept visible.
- Methods & Standards — Operational guidance intended for bounded practitioner use.
- Research Programs — Formal and cross-domain manuscripts tied to unresolved validation or proof burdens.
- Learning & Visuals — Explanatory representations that increase access without flattening uncertainty.

## About

- The Lab — Institutional identity, scope, and present operating boundary.
- How We Work — The construction, evidence, review, and repair discipline used across the Lab.
- Provenance — Where the work came from and which sources support its lineage.
- Contact — The boundary for collaboration, pilots, engineering, and public-interest engagement.
